# Britannia Industries: Did Profit Grow Faster Than Revenue?

Standalone financial analysis, FY2023-24 to FY2025-26 (₹ crore), built in Excel.

## Question
Did Britannia's profit grow faster than its revenue, and what moved?

## Data source
Britannia Industries' standalone annual reports for FY2023-24, FY2024-25 and
FY2025-26. Page references for every figure are in the Data sheet.
[Add links to the three annual reports here.]

## Method
The Data sheet holds the figures from the statements. The Analysis sheet has a
common-size P&L (each line as a % of revenue), year-on-year growth, a profit
bridge for each year, and six ratios: gross margin, operating margin, net
margin, debtor days, inventory days and operating cash flow vs profit.
Operating margin = (revenue - total expenses + finance costs) / revenue, so it
excludes other income and exceptional items. The Checks sheet reconciles the
statements; all checks pass. The Dashboard summarises the results.

## Findings
1. **Profit grew slower, then faster, than revenue.** FY2024-25: profit +2.34%
   vs revenue +6.86%. FY2025-26: profit +20.23% vs revenue +6.65%.
2. **In FY2025-26, revenue growth exceeded expense growth.** Revenue rose by
   ₹1,149.90 crore and total expenses by ₹816.88 crore, a ₹333.02 crore
   difference. Profit rose by ₹431.00 crore; the rest came from other income,
   exceptional items and tax.
3. **Cost structure changed.** Materials' share of revenue rose 4.1 percentage
   points in FY2024-25, then fell 0.7 points in FY2025-26, when purchases fell
   0.5 points and employee benefits rose 0.4 points.
4. Net margin: 12.32% in FY2024-25 and 13.89% in FY2025-26.

The statements show which lines moved, not why.

## Limitations
- Three years of data give two years of change, which is not a trend.
- Debtor and inventory days use year-end balances, not averages.
- FY2024-25 operating cash flow was restated between reports (2,373.15 vs
  2,365.53); the newer figure is used.
- Standalone statements only, not consolidated.
